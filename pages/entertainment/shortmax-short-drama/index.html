What is ShortMax?+

ShortMax is an entertainment app from SHORTMAX LIMITED for short dramas, reels, and TV videos. Its short episodes make it easy to fit a story into a break.

Is everything free to watch?+

The app contains ads and in-app purchases. Some content or features may require payment. Check the app for current access options, prices, and any subscription renewal terms before purchasing.

Can I watch offline?+

ShortMax advertises an offline download feature. Check the app for eligible episodes, access requirements, and download availability.

Where do I get help with a purchase?+

Use Feedback in the app or email shorttv.service@shorttv.live. For subscriptions, check the billing provider shown on your receipt for management and cancellation options.
