Frequently asked questions

What is SaveOnInsurance?

SaveOnInsurance is a free comparison service that helps you see real-time quotes from 120+ insurers, including GEICO, Allstate, and Progressive, so you can compare policies and save up to $700.* We are not an insurance company or agency — we connect you with licensed providers who can quote and issue your policy.

How does it work?

You fill out a simple form, and our comparison tool pulls personalized quotes from our insurer partners. You can then compare those options side by side against your current coverage and choose the one that fits you best.

What affects car insurance rates?

Nationwide, the average cost of car insurance is $187 per month for full coverage and $98 for liability-only, according to industry data. Your costs may be higher or lower based on your driving record (past accidents, speeding tickets, DUIs, and citations), your location, your vehicle, and the coverage levels you choose.

Is my personal information protected and secure?

Our strict no-spam policy ensures your personal details stay secure and private. We'll never sell your data, so you won't get spam emails or unwanted phone calls from comparing rates here.

Do I need to give a phone number to get a quote?

No. You don't need to provide a phone number to compare quotes. You may need to provide one once you decide to finalize a quote and purchase with one of our partners.

Why is it important to compare car insurance quotes?

Insurance companies base rates on common factors like age, location, and driving history — but how they weigh those factors varies a lot. Two insurers may give very different quotes for the same driver, so comparing several is the best way to find your lowest rate.

Who actually issues the policy?

SaveOnInsurance is a comparison service, not an insurance company or agency. Every quote you see comes from a licensed insurer or agent, and they issue and service the policy you choose.

How does this service make money?

Like other comparison services, we may earn a referral fee if you buy a policy through a quote we showed you. We don't make money by selling your data, and comparing costs you nothing.

Which kinds of insurance can I compare?

You can compare car insurance and home insurance, including bundling discounts when you cover both with the same insurer.

What's the best way to lower the cost of auto insurance?

Use an online quote-comparison tool. You can view quotes from multiple companies without visiting each insurer's website, then complete your purchase online or with an agent.
